The real problem

Most med spas don’t have a lead problem. They have a handoff problem.

You can pay for great traffic and still lose the patient between the ad, the front desk, the consult and the next visit. We build the operating layer that closes those gaps.

Leads wait too long
New inquiries sit in an inbox while intent cools. The system assigns, alerts and starts follow-up immediately.
Booked ≠ showed
Without confirmation, reminders and easy rescheduling, the calendar looks full but treatment rooms aren’t.
Consults don’t close
“I’ll think about it” is usually where revenue disappears. We create structured follow-up and objection tracking.
Old patients go quiet
Thousands of past patients and old leads often sit untouched. We segment and reactivate them intelligently.
Memberships leak
Missed payments, cancellations and weak member communication quietly reduce recurring revenue.
Marketing can’t prove revenue
CPL alone hides the truth. We connect source → appointment → show → treatment → revenue where possible.

The system

One revenue engine. Ten connected systems.

Each module has a trigger, workflow, owner, fallback and KPI. The goal isn’t more automation — it’s fewer places for revenue to disappear.

  1. 01 Lead Capture + Attribution Meta, Google, website, phone and referral leads enter one system with source, service interest, campaign and lifecycle data. Ads → CRM
  2. 02 Speed-to-Lead Routing Round-robin assignment, instant alerts, call tasks, SLA tracking and escalation so inquiries are acted on fast. CRM → Team
  3. 03 Qualification + Smart Follow-Up Service, urgency, location and intent determine which person, calendar and nurture path the lead enters. Lead → Path
  4. 04 Booking + No-Show Prevention Service-specific calendars, confirmations, reminders and low-friction rescheduling protect booked revenue. Book → Show
  5. 05 No-Show + Cancellation Recovery Missed appointments trigger a recovery journey instead of becoming dead opportunities. Loss → Recover
  6. 06 Consult-to-Treatment Follow-Up Track consult outcomes, objections and next steps. Follow-up changes based on what actually blocked the sale. Consult → Sale
  7. 07 Rebooking + Reactivation Past patients and old leads receive context-aware follow-up based on relationship, prior service and timing. Database → Revenue
  8. 08 Membership Lifecycle Member onboarding, billing events, renewal touchpoints, failed-payment workflows and cancellation insights. LTV
  9. 09 Reputation + Referral Post-visit feedback, review requests, service-recovery handoffs and referral capture become systematic. Trust
  10. 10 Revenue Reporting + Ad Feedback Report beyond CPL: response time, booking rate, show rate, conversion, CAC, reactivation and attributable revenue. Data → Decisions
