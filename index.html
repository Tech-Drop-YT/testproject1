Designed to connect the systems already running your med spa

The real problem

Most med spas don’t have a lead problem. They have a handoff problem.

You can pay for great traffic and still lose the patient between the ad, the front desk, the consult and the next visit. We build the operating layer that closes those gaps.

Leads wait too long

New inquiries sit in an inbox while intent cools. The system assigns, alerts and starts follow-up immediately.

Booked ≠ showed

Without confirmation, reminders and easy rescheduling, the calendar looks full but treatment rooms aren’t.

Consults don’t close

“I’ll think about it” is usually where revenue disappears. We create structured follow-up and objection tracking.

Old patients go quiet

Thousands of past patients and old leads often sit untouched. We segment and reactivate them intelligently.

Memberships leak

Missed payments, cancellations and weak member communication quietly reduce recurring revenue.

Marketing can’t prove revenue

CPL alone hides the truth. We connect source → appointment → show → treatment → revenue where possible.

The system

One revenue engine. Ten connected systems.

Each module has a trigger, workflow, owner, fallback and KPI. The goal isn’t more automation — it’s fewer places for revenue to disappear.
